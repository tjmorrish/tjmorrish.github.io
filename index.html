Hi — I’m Tom.

I'm a MPhil/PhD student in Statistics, at the University of Warwick. I have a background in Maths (with a BSc from the University of Bath) and Quantitative Finance (with an MSc from Bocconi University, specialising in Quantitative Finance).

My current research focuses on developing novel methods for robust statistical inference for complex time series data. I am particularly interested in applications of these methods to problems in Biology and Ecology, where data are noisy and exhibit non-trivial correlation structure.

When I'm not doing statistics, I can be found walking my 1 year old dog, Louis, or playing tennis for the best tennis club in the world, Chantry Tennis Club.
